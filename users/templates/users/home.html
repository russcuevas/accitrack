PROCESS

How It Works

Three simple steps to report an accident and track your case with the local police.

01
Fill Out the Report

Provide the incident details — location, time, type of accident, severity, and any witness information. Attach photos or videos as evidence.

02
Submit & Receive Case ID

Your report is submitted directly to the police dispatch system. You'll receive a unique case reference number to track your submission.

03
Track Case Progress

Monitor status updates in real time — from open to in-review to closed. Get notified when an officer is assigned or when your case is resolved.
