PROCESS

How It Works

Three simple steps to report an accident and track your case with the local police.

01
Fill Out the Report

Provide the incident details — location, time, type of accident, severity, and any witness information. Attach photos or videos as evidence.

02
Submit & Receive Case ID

Your report is submitted directly to the police dispatch system. You'll receive a unique case reference number to track your submission.

03
Track Case Progress

Monitor status updates in real time — from open to in-review to closed. Get notified when an officer is assigned or when your case is resolved.

Cybercrime Prevention Act & Anti-False Reporting

Filing a false accident report or spreading fake news is strictly prohibited and is punishable by law.

Under the Cybercrime Prevention Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10175) and other relevant Philippine laws, malicious dissemination of false information and submitting fabricated reports through digital systems can lead to:

  • Imprisonment or severe criminal charges.
  • Significant financial fines and penalties.
  • Civil liabilities for damages caused to authorities and affected individuals.

The Philippine National Police (PNP) actively investigates all submitted reports. Any attempt to abuse this system will be traced and prosecuted to the fullest extent of the law.

By proceeding to submit a report, you certify that all information provided is true, accurate, and submitted in good faith.
